How the website works (the big idea)

The website has two sides:

SideWho uses itWhat it does
Public websiteStudents, parents, visitors — anyoneShows school news, teachers, programs, budget, and more. People can only look.
Admin pageOnly school staff who were given accessThe place where you add, change, or remove what the public sees.

Think of it like a bulletin board. The public website is the board everyone reads. The Admin page is the key to the glass case — only trusted people can open it and change the posters.

The most important thing to knowWhen you save something on the Admin page, it shows on the public website right away. You do not need to call anyone or wait for a programmer.

Part 1

The public website

This part is for everyone who visits the school website.

Moving around the website

At the top of every page is the menu bar. It stays there even when you scroll down.

  • Home — the front page.
  • About JOLNHS — point at it (or tap it) to see more choices: Overview and Faculty & Staff.
  • Academics — shows the school programs. Point at it to pick one program.
  • Campus Life — student activities. Point at it to pick a group.
  • Budget Transparency — where the school money goes.
  • News — school announcements.
  • Enroll — the dark blue button. Click it to learn how to enroll.

Below the top part of most pages you will see a small line like Home › Campus Life › Athletes. This shows where you are. Click any word in it to go back to that page.

At the bottom of every page (the footer) you will find the school address, phone, email, office hours, a map, and quick links like Downloads and Contact.

Lost?Click the school logo at the top-left corner. It always brings you back to the Home page.

Campus Life pages, a closer look

  • Organizations, Athletes, Journalists — each group (like SSLG, Basketball, or the school paper) is shown as a card. Click the card to open it and see its officers, coaches, players, and photos. Click again to close it.
  • PTA — shows the PTA officers in a simple list.
  • Gallery — a wall of school photos in different sizes, like Pinterest.
About student privacyFor sports teams, players are shown with name and position only — no photos. This protects our students because they are minors.

Changing the colors

At the bottom-right corner of the screen there is a small round button with a paint palette picture. Click it to choose a color style:

  • Light — white background (the normal look).
  • Dark — dark background, easier on the eyes at night.
  • Sky Blue — a light blue look.

The website remembers your choice the next time you visit on the same device.

The other round button with an arrow pointing up takes you back to the top of the page.

The cookie message

The first time you visit, a small box asks about cookies. This is only about the Google Map at the bottom of the page.

  • Press Accept to show the map.
  • Press Decline if you do not want it. You can still get directions using the link instead.

Using it on a phone

  • On a phone, the menu is hidden. Tap the three lines (☰) at the top-right to open it.
  • Tap a menu item with an arrow to see more choices under it.
  • The Enroll button, Downloads, and Contact Us are at the bottom of the open menu.
  • Tap the X or tap outside the menu to close it.

Part 2

The Admin page

This part is only for school staff who were given admin access. The Admin page is where you change what the public sees.

Logging in

  1. Go to the website address and add /admin at the end. You can also scroll to the very bottom of any page and click the small Admin Login link.
  2. Type your Email and Password.
  3. Press Sign in.
If you type the wrong password 5 timesThe login will stop you for 1 minute. This keeps strangers from guessing passwords. Just wait 1 minute and try again carefully.
"You do not have access" message?Having an account is not enough — another admin must also add you on the Admin Users page (see later). Ask the main admin.

Logging out — and when the website logs you out by itself

  • To log out, press the Sign out button in the admin menu.
  • If you do nothing for 30 minutes, you are logged out by itself.
  • If you close the browser tab, you are logged out too.

This is for safety. If you forget to log out on a shared school computer, no one else can use your access.

TipSave your work often. If you are logged out by the 30-minute rule, unsaved typing is lost.

The Dashboard

This is the first page you see after logging in. It shows a summary: how many staff, Campus Life groups, and budget items there are, plus quick buttons like Add staff and Update budget. Use it as your starting point.

Announcements (News)

Shows on: the News page and the "Latest updates" part of the Home page.

To add a new announcement:

  1. Open Announcements and press the button to add a new one.
  2. Fill in the Title.
  3. Pick a Category (for example, Event or Advisory).
  4. Add a Cover image if you want (not required).
  5. Write a short Excerpt — 1 or 2 sentences shown on the news card.
  6. Write the full message in Body.
  7. Tick Published — visible on the public site.
  8. Press Save.
Your news does not show on the website?Most likely it is still a Draft. Only items with the Published box ticked can be seen by the public. Edit it, tick the box, and save again.
  • Pin — keeps an important announcement at the top of the list.
  • Filipino version — you may also type a Filipino title and message. It shows under the English one.
  • Archive — hides an old announcement without deleting it forever. You can bring it back from the Archived tab.

Staff & Faculty

Shows on: About › Faculty & Staff.

  1. Press Add Staff.
  2. Type the person's name and position.
  3. Pick their group: Administrators, JHS Faculty, SHS Faculty, or Staff.
  4. Pick their Department if they belong to one — for example, Science Department.
  5. Add a photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
  6. Press Save.
  • Departments first. On each tab there is a Departments box — that is where you add the departments (one name at a time). A person can then be assigned to a department inside their form.
  • Department Head. Tick Department Head in a person's form to make them the head. Each department can only have one head — the previous head is replaced automatically.
  • What visitors see. On the public page, departments show as cards that open when clicked, with the head shown first. People with no department sit in an Other card.
  • To change someone, click their row, edit, and save.
  • If a teacher leaves, use Archive instead of deleting. You can see archived people again using the filter at the top.
  • Use the search box to find someone fast.
  • Export to CSV downloads the staff list as a file you can open in Excel.
Departments missing in the form?Add the department in the Departments box first — a person's Department list only shows the ones you already created.
For nice-looking photosUse photos where the person faces the camera, from the shoulders up, with a plain background.

Campus Life

Shows on: the Campus Life pages. At the top there are tabs: Athletes, PTA, Journalists, Organizations. Pick the tab you want to change.

Each tab has these parts:

1. Section information — the title, short tagline, description, and the numbers shown in the "By the Numbers" box (for example, "Student Athletes — 180+").

2. Highlights — the cards under "What We Do" on the public page. Each card has:

  • a title and a description, and
  • an image (not required). Click the small picture box on the left of the card to add one. Click Replace to change it, or the small ✕ to remove it.

Press Add highlight for a new card. Press the trash button to remove one.

Very important — press Save ChangesThe Save Changes button is in the Section information box. It saves the section text, the numbers, and the highlight cards all at once. If you leave without pressing it, your changes are lost (the page will warn you first).

3. Groups (Organizations, Athletes, Journalists only)

TabYou add a…Inside each one you add…
OrganizationsOrganization (like SSLG)Advisers and Officers, with photos
AthletesSport (like Basketball)Coaches with photos, and Players (name and position only, no photos)
JournalistsPublication (like the school paper)Adviser and Staff, with photos
  1. Press the button to add a new group. Type its name, a short description, and add a cover picture.
  2. Save it. Then click the group to open it.
  3. Inside, add its people. For each person type the name and position (for example, "President").
  4. Add photos to the group's photo strip (up to 12) — team photos, events, and so on.
The order of officers is automaticThe website puts people in order by their position: Adviser → President → Vice President → Secretary → Treasurer → Auditor → P.I.O. → Grade Representatives (Grade 7 to 12) → Committees. You do not need to arrange them by hand. Just type the position clearly.

4. People / Officers (PTA tab only) — a simple list of PTA officers with name, position, and photo.

Gallery

Shows on: the photo part of each Campus Life page. Photos in the Campus Gallery tab show on the Gallery page as a Pinterest-style wall.

  1. Open Gallery.
  2. Click the tab of the section the photos belong to.
  3. Press Upload photos. You can pick many photos at once.
  4. Under each photo, type a caption if you want, then press Save.
  • Photos must be JPG, PNG, or WebP and 5 MB or smaller each.
  • Tall and wide photos are both fine — the Gallery wall shows each one in its own shape.
  • To remove a photo, press the trash button and confirm.

Documents (Downloads)

Shows on: the Downloads page.

  1. Open Documents.
  2. Type the Title (for example, "Enrollment Form 2025").
  3. Pick a Category.
  4. Choose the File — PDF, Word, or image, up to 20 MB.
  5. Add a short Description if you want.
  6. Press Publish.

To take down an old file, use Archive.

Budget

Shows on: the Budget Transparency page. The budget is organized by fiscal year (school money year).

  1. Press New Fiscal Year to start a new year (for example, "FY 2025").
  2. In Fiscal year details, type the total budget and the short texts shown on top of the public page.
  3. In Budget categories, add each category (for example, "Learning Materials") with its amount.
  4. Press Set as current year on the year you want the public to see. The public Budget page shows only the current year.
  5. Set the year's status to keep your records in order:
    • Draft — still being worked on.
    • Published / Active — finished and in use.
    • Archived — an old year. It is locked and cannot be changed.
Do not set a year as "current" until it is readyAs soon as a year is the current year, the public can see it — even if its status still says Draft. Finish typing the numbers first, then press Set as current year.
Why some years cannot be editedOld (archived) years are locked on purpose so past records stay honest and are not changed by mistake.

Messages

When someone uses the Contact form on the public website, the message comes here.

  • New messages are marked unread. The top of the Messages page shows how many are unread (for example, "3 unread").
  • Click a message to read it.
  • Press Reply via email to answer — this opens your email app with the sender's address already filled in.
  • Press Archive when you are done, to keep the inbox clean.

Admin Users

This is where you decide who can use the Admin page.

  1. The new person must first have a login account (email and password).
  2. In Grant admin access, type their email and confirm.
  • To remove someone's access, press remove beside their name.
  • You cannot remove yourself. This stops the school from being locked out by mistake.
Give access carefullyEvery admin can change the public website. Only add people the school trusts. Remove access right away when someone leaves their job.

Activity Log

A list of who changed what, and when. Every save on the Admin page is written here — for example, "Maria updated staff Juan Cruz, 10:15 AM." Use the filters to look for a certain person or type of change. It is useful when you need to know who made a change.

Settings

Changes for the whole website:

  • Site Identity — the school name shown on the site.
  • Contact Information — address, phone, email (shows in the footer and Contact page).
  • School Information — other school details.
  • Page Content — the big welcome words on the Home page, the welcome message, the About page history, and the mission statement.

Press Save after changing anything. If you leave a box empty, the website shows its original text instead.

Good habits and safety

  • Never share your password. Each admin should have their own account.
  • Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
  • Check your work on the public website after saving. Open the page and press Ctrl + F5 to refresh.
  • Use Archive, not delete, when possible. Archived things can be brought back.
  • Protect student privacy. Do not post students' home addresses, phone numbers, or birthdays. Use only photos the school is allowed to share.
  • Use clear, small photos. Big photos make the website slow. Keep them under 5 MB.
  • Check spelling of names and positions — this is what parents and students will read.

When something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
I saved something but it does not show on the website.1) For news, make sure it is Published (not Draft). For budget, make sure the year is set as the current year. 2) For Campus Life, make sure you pressed Save Changes. 3) Refresh the public page with Ctrl + F5.
I cannot log in.Check your email and password spelling. If you tried 5 times, wait 1 minute. If it says you have no access, ask the main admin to add you in Admin Users.
I was logged out suddenly.You were inactive for 30 minutes, or the tab was closed. Log in again.
My photo will not upload.Use JPG, PNG, or WebP and make it smaller than 5 MB. You can make a photo smaller by taking a screenshot of it or using a photo app.
A page shows a red error with "Try again".Press Try again. Check your internet. If it keeps happening, tell the person who manages the website.
I made a mistake.Edit it and save again. If you archived something by mistake, bring it back from the Archived view. The Activity Log shows what was changed.
Someone's name appears twice.Open that group, find the extra copy, and archive or remove it.
